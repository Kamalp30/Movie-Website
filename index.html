News & Events

Adventure

When you skydive, you jump out of a plane at an altitude of 10,000 feet or more, and freefall for about a minute at speeds of up to 200 km/h. You feel the wind rushing past your face, the adrenaline pumping through your veins, and the exhilaration of being alive. You see the world from a different perspective, as you soar through the clouds and admire the breathtaking views.

Worth Flight

Skydiving is good for your health and well-being. It can reduce stress, anxiety, and depression. It can also improve your physical fitness, as it involves strength, endurance, and coordination. Skydiving can enhance your mental and physical health.

Memorable Fly

In 2006, a group of 440 skydivers from 31 countries gathered in Thailand to attempt the world record for the largest skydiving formation. They used six military planes, 300 tons of jet fuel, and a trainload of oxygen bottles to reach an altitude of 25,000 feet. After several tries, they managed to form a 400-way formation that held for 4.25 seconds, breaking the previous record of 357-way. The event was a tribute to the 60th anniversary of the rule of Thailand’s King Bhumibol Adulyadej
